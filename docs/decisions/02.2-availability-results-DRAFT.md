## 1. Section objective

Turn a search from the booking bar into a chosen room and rate, on VNest's own site, with no surprise about price; and where the guest has not chosen a property, help them choose one by its character as well as its availability.

## 2. Design as built

One route, `/stays`, two journeys decided by the URL.

**Journey A, no property in the URL.** One card per property: signature visual (5:4 on a phone, 8:9 in two columns, 6:5 in three, so the picture leads and the first row's price stays in the first screen), area, name, one line of positioning, up to three signature amenities, availability status, the total for the stay with the room it belongs to and its tax status, and one action, "Xem phòng". One column on a phone, two from 768px, three from 1200px; no card is larger than another. Available properties come first, then properties that could not be checked, then confirmed sold-out properties in a compact list. Sorting by total price is offered when at least two prices exist.

**Journey B, a property in the URL.** A strip of identity (small visual, area, name, the stay), then rooms. Each bookable room lists up to three rates as radio options, with the rest behind "more rates"; each rate shows the provider's inclusions and terms beside its total. Unavailable rooms follow in a compact list with the reason. Choosing a rate shows a summary with "Tiếp tục đặt": a bar fixed to the bottom on a phone, a panel beside the rooms from 1024px.

**Loading and order.** While the provider is asked, the page shows one outline per property, the size of the final card. The real cards then appear once, already in order: available, then properties that could not be checked, with confirmed sold-out properties in their own list after. That order is fixed for the search. A property retried later is updated where it stands, even if it turns out to be sold out; nothing the guest is looking at moves. Only a new search, a refresh or the guest's own sort reorders. The server gives up on a silent provider after 12 seconds and reports a timeout.

**A missing price.** A price the provider did not verify is never shown as a rate. On a card: "Chưa có giá", no figure. In Journey B: the room is listed as available with no radio and no figure. If the provider declares a hand-off, the room can be selected as a room ("Chọn phòng này", then "Tiếp tục với phòng này"), and the summary says it is a room, not a rate. If it declares none, nothing is offered to continue to. Nothing says a later step will show a price.

**Five states, never confused:**

| State | Mark and words | Shown when |
|---|---|---|
| Available | Green dot, "Còn phòng" | The provider confirmed a room the party can book |
| Sold out | Hollow ring, "Hết phòng" | The provider said sold out for every room |
| Unknown | Dashed ring, "Chưa rõ phòng trống" | No answer, not connected, no usable offer, or no room takes the party |
| Provider error | Red square, "Chưa kiểm tra được", with "Thử lại" | The request for that property, or the whole search, failed |
| Stale | Amber diamond, "Kết quả cũ", notice with "Cập nhật giá" | The answer's validity or the site's 15-minute limit has passed |

A failed request is never shown as sold out: `getPropertyOutcome` puts a failure before any answer, and tests cover it at domain, derivation and rendered-page level.

**Visual language.** Follows the approved booking bar: warm surfaces, 14px outer corners, hairlines, small tracked labels. Cards are unboxed: picture, type and a hairline, not a bordered tile. Copper is used once per page, on "Tiếp tục đặt".

## 3. Why this design

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Property-led cards, not a room list | With six properties, a flat price list removes the difference VNest is selling |
| Story carried by the card itself | Every element between the guest and a price costs time; the visual, name and one line are things the guest looks at anyway |
| Total for the stay as the headline | It is what the guest pays. The per-night figure appears only on rates, labelled as an average |
| Rates inline under each room | Terms beside the price are the point of choosing a rate |
| Sold-out properties shown, last and quiet | The owner's decision. They answer "is it full or did it fail?" and offer a change of dates |
| Outlines while loading, then a fixed order | Cards a guest has started reading must not change place |
| A missing price is stated, never implied | An unknown price shown as a rate, or a promise that the next step will show one, would be a claim no contract supports |
| Status by mark and words | No state is told apart by colour alone |
| No urgency devices | Units left appear only when the provider states them; there are no countdowns |
| Availability fetched by the browser | A slow PMS never delays the page; retry and refresh need no reload |

## 4. Shared property card

`src/components/property-card/`:

- `CardVisual.tsx`: a picture in a fixed frame; labels stand-ins; draws a quiet block when there is no file.

## 5. Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Trade-off | Why not chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Room-led list across properties | Fastest to the cheapest room | The pattern of a booking marketplace |
| Cards with an inline room preview | Shows what the price buys without a tap | Taller cards; the room name beside the price already says it. Still open as a refinement |
| Rates in a second step | Shorter page | Hides the total and terms behind a tap for every comparison |
| Hiding sold-out properties | Cleaner page | Owner's decision to show them |
| Property content at once, reordered when the answer arrives | Identity visible about a second sooner | Built first. Measured layout shift of 0.14 to 0.24 when any property was sold out. Replaced by outlines on 11/10/2026 at the owner's request |
| Keeping sold-out properties in place, dimmed | No movement at all | Sold-out properties would sit among available ones; the owner asked for available first |
| One `PropertyCard` with every variant now | One file | Would mean building Phase 04 early |

## 7. SEO

- `/stays` is `noindex` with or without parameters; its canonical is `/stays` with no query. Verified in the response of a production build.
- Not in the sitemap. Sort controls are buttons. Links between journeys carry dates and point at the same canonical.
- No price or availability in structured data; the page emits none.
- The route renders on request; no parameter page is generated.

**Proposal, not built: an indexable directory at `/stays`.**

| | Search results | Directory |
|---|---|---|
| Address | `/stays` with any of `checkIn`, `checkOut`, `adults`, `property`, `destination` | `/stays` with no parameters |
| What it is | A tool: an answer for one stay | Content: VNest's properties, each introduced and linked to its page |
| Indexing | `noindex, follow`, always | Indexable only when the deployment is indexable **and** every listed property is publishable (`isPublishable`: not sample data, approved by VNest) |
| Canonical | `/stays` | `/stays` |
| Sitemap | Never | Listed once the rule above holds |
| Structured data | None | An `ItemList` of the property pages; no price, availability or rating |

Until real, approved property records exist, the directory rule cannot be true, so the bare page stays `noindex` as it is today and nothing fabricated can be published. It needs property pages (Phase 05) to link to, and its own title, description and introductory copy from VNest. Recommended: decide in Phase 04, with the discovery cards, since the directory is where they would live.

## 8. Accessibility and performance

| | Decision |
|---|---|
| Rates | Native radio inputs sharing one name inside a `fieldset` per room with a `legend`. Space chooses, arrows move across rooms, Enter continues |
| Announcements | A `role="status"` region states the result in words; the selection summary has its own |
| Targets | Every link, button and rate row is 44px or more at 390px (measured) |
| Motion | No animation added beyond the loading pulse, which stops under reduced motion |
| First response | Renders property content without waiting for a provider |
| Requests | One per search; cancelled when the search changes; no polling; one timer for staleness |
| Images | `next/image` in fixed frames; the first row loads eagerly, the rest lazily |
| Layout stability | Outlines of final size while loading; cards built in place, in final order; order fixed per search |

**JavaScript, measured on the production build.** `/stays` loads 206.4 KB gzip of script; the booking-bar preview route loads 187.3 KB; the difference is **19.1 KB**, against the specification's 15 KB.

| Part of the difference | gzip (pro rata within its chunk) |
|---|---|
| Section 02.2's own code: both journeys, the availability hook, derivation rules, wording | about 14.9 KB |
| `next/image` | about 3.2 KB |
| Small shared modules in the same chunk, not identified individually | about 1.7 KB |
| A second chunk: `next/link` (1.4 KB) and other framework helpers (2.2 KB) | 3.6 KB |
| Less: a chunk only the booking-bar preview page loads | 4.2 KB |

Removed: one unused string. No dependency was added by this section, and nothing else in it was found unused.

**A revised budget is requested: 20 KB.** The 15 KB figure was set before the section had five distinct states, per-property retry, stale-price handling and the room-versus-rate distinction, all of which the owner has since required; the section's own code alone is at the old budget. Two ways to go lower exist if the owner prefers to keep 15 KB, neither done: load each journey's code only on that journey (estimated 4 to 6 KB less per page, at the cost of a short wait when moving from results to rooms); and compute image attributes on the server instead of shipping `next/image` (about 3 KB, at the cost of maintaining our own image markup).

## 9. Component architecture and data flow

```
/stays (server)  reads the URL, lists properties, validates the stay, renders content
   └ StaysExperience (browser)
        ├ StaySearchProvider + ResultsSearchBar   frozen 02.1 bar, compact, unchanged
        ├ useAvailability        one request, kept above both journeys
        ├ AllPropertiesResults   Journey A
        └ PropertyRooms          Journey B

GET /api/availability → checkAvailability → BookingProvider.searchAvailability
```

- `derive.ts` holds every rule (status, "from" price, order, room states, selection) as pure functions. Components present.
- `useAvailability` keeps the last answer. Journey A to B for the same stay needs no request; a failed property is retried alone and merged with `mergeAnswers`.
- No component imports an adapter; no provider name appears outside `src/integrations`.
- "Change dates" opens the frozen booking bar by pressing its own control (`search-control.ts`).

Remaining limitations:

1. **JavaScript is over the original budget**; a decision on the revised figure is needed.
2. **Property identity now appears with the answer, not before it.** That is the price of a stable layout: with a slow provider the guest sees outlines until the answer or the 12-second bound.
3. **Per-property time bounds are a rule for adapters, not something the mock exercises.** The whole-search bound is tested.
4. **Sorting by price shows no visible change with the fixtures**, because simulated prices rise in VNest's order. The rule is unit-tested.
5. The 15-minute price age limit and the 12-second provider bound are provisional assumptions, not PMS rules.
6. "Tiếp tục đặt" and "Tiếp tục với phòng này" stop at a notice: hand-off is section 02.3. How a room without an offer price is handed over is undefined until a provider documents it.
7. Property cards cannot link to property pages until Phase 05; "Xem phòng" opens Journey B.
8. The experience-led card can be judged for structure only until real photography exists.
9. Interface wording is the build team's draft.
10. "Change dates" presses the frozen bar's own control, because the bar exposes no way to open it from outside.
11. The "not connected" state (`BOOKING_PROVIDER=none`) is covered by tests, not by a screenshot.
12. The repository tracks generated files on `main`; see `docs/REPOSITORY-AUDIT.md`.

Awaiting VNest: the six properties and their approved photography; signature amenities; room capacities, bedding and areas; approved copy; logo, colour and typeface; a contact route for properties without online booking; provider documentation.

VNest V2 · Property experience · Art-direction proposal · 12/10/2026

Living Frames

A property is shown as a small set of frames, each one a moment of staying there. One of them moves. All of them are labelled with where and when. The booking action is always one of the things on screen.

Proposal · not approved · nothing implemented

What this document is

  • Two compositions, Cinematic Arrival and Motion Contact Sheet, each as a first screen at 1440px and at 390px, with a storyboard for its showcase clip.
  • Static design boards only. No route, component or playback was built, and the earlier A, B and B+ prototypes are untouched.
  • Every picture here is a drawing made for this proposal. None is VNest photography or footage, the property name and place are the sample record's, and the hours on the frames are examples of the format, not facts.

1. The system both directions share

Four rules make a page recognisably VNest whichever composition is chosen.

Three frame shapes
Wide 16:9 for atmosphere, upright 4:5 for a room or a person, square for a detail. Nothing else, so stills and clips can swap places.
Every frame is stamped

02·17:55·Phòng ngủ

Number, hour, place, in one small line outside the picture. The hours turn a gallery into a day. They are printed only when the shoot recorded them.
One frame moves

● Đang phát 00:04 / 00:18

The showcase clip plays muted by itself. Any other clip waits for the guest. A moving frame shows a progress hairline and one pause control; a waiting one shows a play mark.
One action
Xem phòng trống
“Xem phòng trống” is the only filled colour on the screen. Words are never set on a picture, so contrast never depends on the footage.

2. Direction 1 — Cinematic Arrival

A dark room with one large moving frame running off the edge of the screen, two smaller frames stepping up to it, and the name set very large underneath.

First screen, 1440px

What makes it recognisable

  • The staircase of frames. Square, upright, wide: three sizes stepping from the left margin up to the showcase, each cutting into the next. It is one shape, not a hero with thumbnails beside it.
  • The frame leaves the screen. The showcase runs off the right edge, so it reads as a window onto something larger than the page.
  • The name is the floor. It is the largest thing after the picture and it sits on the dark ground under the frames, never on the footage.
  • Dark only here. The page returns to the site's light surface below the first screen; the dark room is the arrival, not the theme.

Interaction

  • Frame 01 plays muted and loops once the poster has painted. The pause control stops it and stays stopped.
  • Frame 03 is a three-second loop that plays while the pointer is on it, or after one tap on a phone, then returns to its still.
  • Any frame, and “Xem cả 8 khung hình”, opens the full gallery at that frame. Nothing swaps places and nothing scrolls sideways.
  • “Xem phòng trống” is a plain link to the rooms on the same page, as today. It works before any script or video has loaded.
  • With reduced motion, data saver or a failed autoplay: frame 01 is its poster with a play mark. Nothing else changes.

3. Direction 2 — Motion Contact Sheet

A photographer's sheet on paper: frames of three shapes on one strict grid, one of them marked and moving, the name and the booking action sitting in the grid as frames of their own.

First screen, 1440px

What makes it recognisable

  • The sheet. Every frame sits on the same twelve-column grid with the same gutter and its stamp underneath. Wide, upright and square are mixed, but nothing overlaps and nothing is cropped by the screen.
  • The marked frame. Four copper corners pick out the one that moves, the way an editor marks the chosen shot. Without motion it is still the obvious first thing to look at.
  • The action is a frame. “Xem phòng trống” fills a cell of the sheet and carries the guest's own dates. It is the only solid colour on the paper.
  • The name is a frame too. It takes a cell of its own at the size of a picture.

Interaction

  • Frame 01 plays muted by itself. No other frame moves unless asked.
  • Frames with a play mark play a three-second loop while the pointer is on them. On a phone they play once when they are fully on screen and the guest has stopped scrolling, one at a time, and never with reduced motion or data saver.
  • Any frame opens the full gallery at that frame; “+3 khung hình” opens it at the first one not on the sheet.
  • The action tile is a plain link to the rooms. Its dates come from the URL, as the criteria bar's do; with no dates it reads “Chọn ngày”.

4. Typography and hierarchy

Two typefaces. The name is set in a narrow, heavy grotesque at a size that competes with the pictures; everything else is the site's body face.

LevelSetting
1 · NameBricolage Grotesque, narrow, 800. Up to 150px at 1440 and 76px at 390. Sentence case, line height 1 or more so stacked Vietnamese marks never collide.
2 · ActionBe Vietnam Pro 600, 16px, white on copper (5.25:1). The only filled colour.
3 · Place, statementBe Vietnam Pro 500 and 400, 14 to 16px. One line of place, one sentence of statement, no more.
4 · StampBe Vietnam Pro 500, 11px, capitals, wide tracking, tabular figures. Always outside the picture.

Provisional, like every typeface in V2: VNest's identity has not been supplied. Both are openly licensed (SIL OFL) and drawn with Vietnamese marks.

5. Video: placement, shapes and behaviour

Shapes and responsive behaviour

  • Each showcase is delivered as two files, a wide master and an upright cut, chosen by the shape of the frame and not only by screen width. A phone never downloads the wide file.
  • Shot wide with the subject inside the centre 4:5, so the upright cut is a crop of the same take where a separate upright take does not exist.
  • In Direction 1 the lower left of the showcase is covered by frame 02. That corner is a no-subject zone in the edit.
  • Posters are stills from the first second of the clip, in both shapes, so play starts without a jump.

Behaviour, both directions

SoundNone. The files carry no audio track and there is no unmute control.
Inlinemuted, playsinline, loop. Never full screen by itself.
Poster firstThe poster is the first screen's largest picture and loads with priority. The clip is requested only after it has painted.
Secondary clipsNot requested at all until the guest points at, taps or settles on their frame.
Reduced motionNo autoplay anywhere. Posters with a play mark; playing is the guest's choice.
PauseOne 44px control on the moving frame, reachable by keyboard, labelled, and it stays paused. Required for any motion longer than five seconds.
When autoplay is refusedLow-power mode, data saver or a browser policy: the poster stays and the play mark appears. The layout is identical.
Off screenPlayback pauses when the frame leaves the screen.
BookingNever depends on video. The name, the place and “Xem phòng trống” are HTML in the first response.

Loading budget (proposed, to be measured on the prototype)

Yesterday's measurement on the B+ prototype is the only evidence so far: with a 188 KB hero picture on a throttled phone connection, the largest picture painted at about 2.7 seconds. So the poster should stay under roughly 150 KB at phone width. The figures for the clips themselves are targets with no measurement behind them yet: about 2 MB for a phone showcase and 400 KB for a detail loop.

Booking actionA conventional button, bottom right at 1440 and full width at 390.A tile in the grid. Distinctive, but a guest has to recognise a tile as a button. Hypothesis to test.
Risk of looking ordinaryLow at 1440. At 390 it is close to “picture, name, button” and relies on the overlap and the stamp for character.A photo mosaic is a familiar booking-site pattern. The stamps, the marked frame and the two text cells have to carry the difference.
Fit with the existing pageThe way-back link sits on the dark ground and needs a light variant. The frozen bar stays light above it; the join is a hard edge.Sits on a light surface like the rest of the site. No change to anything around it.
Long property namesThe name has the full width; a second line pushes the action below the first screen at 390.The name cell is narrow at 1440; a long name drops to a smaller size or takes the statement's place.

Risks common to both

  • The hours are a promise. A stamp that says 06:50 must be true of that shot. If the shoot does not record times, the stamp becomes number and place only, and part of the idea is lost.
  • Nothing here has been seen with real footage or by a guest. That the format feels youthful and editorial is a hypothesis.
  • Video weight is unmeasured. The budgets above are targets.
  • The narrow display face needs its width axis. Loading only the one instance needed is feasible but has not been built, and the font weight must stay within yesterday's 100 KB target.

8. Decisions needed

  1. Which composition goes forward to one isolated prototype.
  2. Whether frames carry an hour, and whether VNest's shoots can supply it.
  3. Whether footage will be shot or cut upright as well as wide.
  4. For Direction 1: accept a dark first screen under the light bar.
  5. For Direction 2: accept the action as a tile, to be tested, or keep a conventional button.
  6. Whether licensed placeholder clips may be sourced for the prototype, or playback is prototyped with drawn frames only.

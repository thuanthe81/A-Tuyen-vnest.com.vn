# 02.2 Availability results and rate selection: section specification

**Status: DIRECTION APPROVED 10/10/2026, implemented, IN REVIEW.** See `docs/decisions/02.2-availability-results-DRAFT.md` for what was built and where it differs from this specification.

Principle: Booking First. Experience Driven. SEO Built-in.

Statements about guests, younger travellers or conversion are **hypotheses** unless evidence is cited. None is validated. No real property, price or availability appears in this document; every example value is a placeholder.

## 1. Business objective

Turn a search into a chosen room and rate, on VNest's own site, with the fewest steps and no surprises about price. Where the guest has not chosen a property, help them choose one by its character as well as its availability.

## 2. Scope

**In:** the page a search from the booking bar lands on; presenting properties, rooms, rates, prices and policies for the searched stay; choosing one rate; every loading, empty, unavailable, error and stale state; handing the chosen rate to section 02.3.

**Out:** hand-off, checkout, payment and confirmation (02.3); property detail pages (Phase 05); the site header (03.1); any real PMS adapter.

## 3. The two journeys

Both start from the frozen booking bar (section 02.1), which already produces the two searches:

| | Journey A: all properties | Journey B: one property |
|---|---|---|
| Arrives with | no `property` in the URL | `property=<slug>` |
| Guest's question | "Where should I stay on these dates?" | "Which room, at what price?" |
| Page answers with | Properties that have a room for the party, each with its best available option | That property's rooms and their rates, immediately |
| Primary action | Choose a property (opens its rooms) | Choose a rate, then continue |
| Storytelling | Present: the property's signature visual, name, area and what is particular to it | Minimal: a compact identity strip. The guest has already chosen |

Journey A leads into Journey B: choosing a property shows that property's rooms for the same dates, without searching again when the answer is already in hand.

Journey B never sends the guest back through discovery. If the property has nothing for the dates, it offers one clear way out: the same dates across all properties.

## 4. Storytelling against booking speed

The tension: character sells a distinctive stay, but every element between the guest and a price costs time.

Position taken: **story is carried by the content the guest must look at anyway, not by added sections.**

- The property's signature visual *is* the card. Name, area and one line of positioning sit on or beside it.
- At most three "signature" amenities are shown, taken from the amenities VNest has flagged as particular to that property. Generic amenities are not listed here.
- Price and the next action are visible without scrolling inside the card and without opening anything.
- No carousel, no autoplaying video and no editorial paragraphs on the results page. Depth lives on the property page (Phase 05), one tap away, carrying the dates.
- In Journey B the story shrinks to a strip (small visual, name, area). Rooms begin in the first screen on a phone.

Hypothesis: for guests choosing among six distinctive properties, one strong image and one line do more than a list of features. Not validated.

## 5. Approaches compared

### Journey A

| | A1. Property-led cards | A2. Room-led list | A3. Property cards with an inline room preview |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shape | One large card per property: visual, identity, "from" price for the stay, one action | A flat list of every available room across properties, sorted by price | A1, plus the best one or two available rooms listed inside the card |
| Strength | Each property keeps its character; six cards are scannable in one scroll | Fastest path to the cheapest room | Shows what the price buys without a tap |
| Weakness | The price has no room attached; one more tap to see rooms | Six properties dissolve into a price table: the pattern of a booking marketplace, and the opposite of the brief | Taller cards; more to load and read on a phone |
| Suits | Guests choosing by place and feel | Guests choosing by price alone | Guests doing both |

**Recommendation: A1, with one line naming the room the "from" price belongs to.** It keeps cards short and distinctive, and the price is never detached from what it buys. A3 remains available as a refinement if review shows guests want rooms sooner. A2 is not recommended: with six properties it adds speed nobody needs and removes the difference VNest is selling.

### Journey B

| | B1. Rates inline under each room | B2. Room cards, rates in a second step |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | Each room row lists its rate options as selectable choices | Each room shows one "from" price; tapping opens a sheet of its rates |
| Strength | Everything comparable on one page; one tap to choose | Shorter page; calmer first impression |
| Weakness | Long when rooms have many rates | An extra step for every comparison; the total is hidden until the sheet opens |

**Recommendation: B1, showing up to three rates per room and folding the rest behind "more rates".** Seeing the terms beside the price is the point of rate selection. If a provider turns out to return many rate plans per room, revisit.

### Where Journey B lives

| | On the results route | On the property page |
|---|---|---|
| Now | Possible: one route serves both journeys | Not possible: property pages arrive in Phase 05 |
| Later | A second place to maintain rooms | The natural home: rooms beside the property's own content |

**Recommendation:** build the room and rate list as a component that needs only a property and a search. In 02.2 it renders on the results route. In Phase 05 the same component is placed on the property page, and whether the results route then redirects single-property searches there is decided with evidence from use.

## 7. Presenting rooms, prices, policies and availability

**Rooms.** Name, one photo, capacity, bedding and area come from VNest's property record, joined to the provider's answer by room type id. A field the record lacks is omitted, never filled in.

**Prices.** Every price shown is traceable to one provider offer.

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| The headline figure is the **total for the stay**, with the number of nights beside it | It is the number the guest pays; a per-night figure invites a surprise at checkout |
| A per-night figure may appear as secondary text, computed as total ÷ nights and labelled as an average | Familiar for comparison; honest about being derived |
| Tax and fee status is always stated: included, not included, or "not stated by the provider" | The contract already distinguishes the three |
| "từ" (from) on a property card is the lowest total among that property's available offers **for the searched stay** | A "from" price for other dates would be a different, weaker claim |
| A price the provider did not supply is shown as "price shown at the next step", never estimated | No invented prices |
| Currency is shown as supplied; no conversion | |

Hypothesis: showing the total and the tax status first builds trust with guests used to price reveals late in checkout. Not validated.

**Policies.** Cancellation and payment terms are shown as the provider's own text beside the rate they belong to. The site writes no policy wording. When a provider supplies none, the rate says "terms shown before you confirm" and nothing more.

**Availability.**

| Provider says | Shown as |
|---|---|
| Available, with offers | Selectable rates |
| Available, no offer | "Available: price shown at the next step" only if hand-off can proceed without one; otherwise treated as unverified |
| Sold out | Listed after available items, compact, labelled sold out for these dates |
| Unknown, or the room cannot take the party | "Not suitable for N guests" with the limit, or omitted when nothing is known |
| Units left | Shown only when the provider states it. Never estimated, never used as pressure |

No urgency devices: no countdowns, no "N people are looking", no "last room" unless it is the provider's stated count.

**Order.** Default order is VNest's own editorial order, with available items first. Sorting by total price is offered when prices exist. Nothing is labelled "recommended" or "popular" without a defined, real basis.

## 8. States

| State | Trigger | What the guest sees | Action offered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loading | Request in flight | Property and room content that the site already holds renders at once; only price and availability areas show placeholders of the final size | None needed |
| Results | Answer received | As sections 6 and 7 | Choose |
| Simulated | The answer's source is mock | A visible "Dữ liệu mô phỏng" label on the page and on every price | As results; continue stops at the development notice |
| Nothing available, all properties | No property has a room | "Không còn phòng cho ngày này" with the stay restated | Change dates (opens the booking bar's calendar) |
| Property sold out (Journey B) | The property has no room | The same message for that property | "Xem các cơ sở khác cho ngày này" (same dates, all properties); change dates |
| Room over capacity | Party exceeds the room's limit | Room listed as not suitable, with its limit | Change guests |
| Not connected | Provider declares no availability, or the property is not mapped | Property content with "Đặt phòng trực tuyến chưa sẵn sàng". No claim about availability | Contact route, once VNest supplies one |
| Error, retryable | Network, timeout or provider fault | A plain message in place of prices; content stays | Thử lại |
| Partial answer | Some properties answered, some failed | Answered properties as normal; failed ones marked "could not check" | Retry the failed ones |
| Stale price | The answer is older than its validity, or older than a set age while the page stayed open | A notice above the results: prices may have changed | Refresh; selection is kept if the offer still exists |
| Offer expired | The chosen offer is missing from a refreshed answer, or hand-off reports it expired | The selection is cleared with a notice naming what changed | Choose again |
| Invalid search | Dates or guests in the URL are invalid | The booking bar open on the problem, with its message. No request is made | Fix the search |
| No dates | URL without dates | Journey A: properties in browse mode with no price or availability claims. Journey B: rooms without rates | Choose dates |

Every state must be reachable in development through a mock scenario, and each is reviewed individually.

## 9. Optional property selection (from section 02.1)

- No `property` parameter is Journey A. It is the default, never an error.
- An unknown or unreadable `property` falls back to Journey A silently, as the booking bar already does.
- The booking bar stays on the page, pre-filled from the URL. Changing the property there switches journey; dates and guests are kept.
- The frozen component is used as it is. This section needs no change to it.

## 10. Component architecture (for implementation, once authorised)

```
src/app/(site)/stays/page.tsx          server: reads the URL, resolves the journey, renders content
src/features/availability/
  derive.ts                            pure: per-property summary, lowest offer, ordering, staleness
  derive.test.ts
  useAvailability.ts                   client: request, cancel, retry, refresh; uses availabilityReducer
  ResultsHeading.tsx                   the answer in words
  PropertyResultCard.tsx               Journey A card
  PropertyStrip.tsx                    Journey B identity strip
  RoomResult.tsx                       one room with its rates
  RateOption.tsx                       one selectable rate: price, terms
  PriceLine.tsx                        the one place a price is formatted and labelled
  SelectionSummary.tsx                 chosen rate and the continue action
  states/                              empty, error, not-connected, stale
src/features/booking-search/           frozen; reused unchanged
```

- **Separation.** `derive.ts` holds every rule about what to show (lowest price, order, stale or not) as pure functions with tests. Components only present.
- **Rendering.** The page renders content the site holds on the server. Availability is fetched in the browser from `/api/availability` through the reducer that already exists in the domain. Reasons: a slow PMS never delays the first response; retry and refresh need no page reload; the same hook will serve property pages.
- **Selection.** The chosen offer lives in page state, not in the URL: an offer reference is short-lived and must not be shared or bookmarked. The search itself stays in the URL.
- **One price formatter.** `PriceLine` is the only component that prints money, so the tax label and the simulated label cannot be forgotten in one place.

## 11. Backend data contract

What the page needs, independent of who supplies it. Types are in `src/domain/availability.ts`.

| Need | Already in the contract | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Search one property or all | `scope`, `propertyIds` | None |
| Per room: status, offers | `RoomAvailability`, `RateOffer` | None |
| Rate plan name and inclusions | `ratePlan` | None |
| Total for the stay, nights, tax status | `Price` (dated) | None |
| Cancellation and payment terms | Plain text per offer | Structured terms (free-cancellation deadline as a date) would allow clearer display; text is enough to start |
| Opaque reference per offer | `OfferRef` | None |
| Provider occupancy limit, units left | Present | None |
| **Per-property outcome** | Absent: a search fails or succeeds as a whole | **Add a status per property** (answered, failed, not connected) so one property's failure does not hide five answers |
| **Freshness** | Absent | **Add when the answer was produced, and until when it is valid** if the provider states it. Needed for the stale-price state |
| **Why unavailable** | Absent | Optional reason per room (for example a minimum-stay rule) so the page can say what would work. Only if a provider reports it |
| Currency | ISO code per amount | Confirm one currency per search |

Questions for ezCloud, GoHost or VNest's backend team, beyond those in `docs/BOOKING.md`:

1. Can several properties be queried in one call, and what is the expected response time for six?
2. How long is a quoted price or offer valid?
3. Is the price a total or per night, and are taxes and fees included, separate, or unstated?
4. Are rate plans, inclusions and cancellation terms returned per rate, and as text or structure?
5. Are remaining units returned?
6. When a stay is unavailable, is the reason returned?
7. What caching is permitted, and what rate limits apply?

These contract additions touch `domain/availability.ts`, which section 02.1 depends on. They are additive, and will be regression-checked against 02.1 under the freeze policy.

## 12. Independence from the PMS

- The page consumes only domain types. No component, hook or derivation imports an adapter or knows a provider's name.
- Each provider's adapter maps its responses into those types inside `src/integrations/booking/<provider>/`.
- A provider without multi-property search is asked once per property inside its adapter; the page cannot tell the difference.
- Capabilities decide what is offered: no `rates` means no prices are shown, not prices invented; no `availability` means the not-connected state.
- Mock data is produced by the mock adapter only, always marked as mock, with obviously artificial figures.

## 13. SEO

The results page is a tool, not content. It must not create indexable pages for arbitrary parameters.

| Rule | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Results are never indexed | `noindex, follow` on the results route whenever it carries dates, guests or a property |
| One canonical | Canonical is the route without parameters |
| No crawl trap | Sort and filter controls are buttons, not links. Links that carry dates point to pages whose canonical has no parameters |
| Not in the sitemap | Only the parameterless route, and only if it has indexable content of its own |
| The API is never indexed or cached | Already so |
| No availability or price in structured data | Already the rule; these values are not stable |
| No statically generated parameter pages | The route renders on request |
| Internal links pass value | Each card links to its property page (Phase 05), which is the indexable page for that property |

Open: whether the parameterless results route should exist as an indexable "all stays" page. Recommended: decide in Phase 04, with the property discovery cards.

## 15. Risks and dependencies

| Risk or dependency | Effect |
|---|---|
| **No real property content or photography** | An experience-led card cannot be judged on placeholders. The pattern can be built and reviewed for structure, not for feel |
| **Overlap with Phase 04 (six property discovery cards)** | The Journey A card and the discovery card are the same object at two densities. Designing them separately would produce two cards for one property. Recommended: define the card's anatomy once here, and let Phase 04 extend it |
| ezCloud capabilities unknown | If it returns no prices or no per-rate terms, rate selection reduces to room selection and a hand-off |
| Response time for six properties unknown | Decides how much loading design matters, and whether results should arrive per property |
| Property pages do not exist | Cards cannot link to them until Phase 05; "Xem phòng" opens Journey B in the meantime |
| Section 02.3 not built | "Tiếp tục đặt" ends at a labelled boundary until hand-off exists |
| Header (03.1) not built | The page ships with the sticky booking bar as its only top element until then |
| Contract additions touch a file the frozen section relies on | Reviewed and regression-tested under the freeze policy |
| Copper and typeface are provisional | The page inherits them |

## 16. Acceptance checklist

Each item is checkable. "Verified" means evidence is recorded in the decision record.

**Journeys**

- [ ] A search with no property shows properties; a search with a property shows that property's rooms in the first screen at 390px.
- [ ] Choosing a property in Journey A shows its rooms for the same stay without a new search when the answer is already held.
- [ ] A sold-out property in Journey B offers the same dates across all properties.
- [ ] Changing the search in the booking bar updates the page and the URL; back and forward restore each search.

**Truthfulness**

- [ ] Every price on screen can be traced to one provider offer in the response; none is computed except the labelled per-night average.
- [ ] Every price states its tax and fee status.
- [ ] Policy text is the provider's own or absent.
- [ ] No urgency device appears; units left appears only when the provider supplies it.
- [ ] Every value from mock data carries a visible simulated label.

**States**

- [ ] All thirteen states in section 8 are reachable through mock scenarios and reviewed.
- [ ] A late response for a changed search is never shown (covered by the existing reducer and its tests).
- [ ] One property failing does not hide the others.

**Quality**

- [ ] Rendered and operated at 390, 768 and 1440px with no truncation or horizontal overflow.
- [ ] Rate selection and continue are completable by keyboard alone; rate options are a labelled group.
- [ ] Results and state changes are announced to assistive technology.
- [ ] Touch targets are 44px or more; text contrast meets WCAG AA.
- [ ] Layout shift between loading and results is 0.02 or less, measured.
- [ ] Added JavaScript is 15 KB gzip or less, measured.
- [ ] Derivation rules have unit tests; lint, typecheck, tests and build pass.

**SEO and boundaries**

- [ ] The results route with parameters returns `noindex, follow` and a parameterless canonical, verified in the response.
- [ ] Sort and filter create no crawlable URLs.
- [ ] No component imports an adapter; no provider name appears outside `src/integrations`.
- [ ] Section 02.1 passes its regression after any contract change.
